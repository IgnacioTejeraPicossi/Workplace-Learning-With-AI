# Self-Simulating Reality Agent — User Guide

The **Self-Simulating Reality Agent** is a serious, epistemically disciplined companion
for the most speculative idea on the roadmap: **the proposition that observers, minds or
consciousness participate in constructing the universe they experience**. Its anchor
framework is **Observer Patch Holography (OPH)** by Bernhard Mueller et al.

> **Read this first — the one rule.** The agent never tells you what to believe. Its
> guiding phrase is: *"I don't tell you what to believe. I show you what is science, what
> is theory, what is philosophy, and what is metaphysical imagination."* Every claim is
> labelled by **evidence level**, and OPH itself is always labelled **speculative** — never
> presented as established physics. The agent never says "this is true"; it says "this
> belongs to level X."

---

## The 5 epistemic levels

Every claim in every tab carries one of these tags (colour-coded badges):

| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Established** | Empirically supported and broadly accepted (e.g. the brain builds predictive models) |
| **Mainstream** | Serious theory, mathematically formulated, not fully settled (e.g. the holographic principle) |
| **Speculative** | Structured speculative programme with its own formalism (e.g. OPH itself) |
| **Philosophy** | Argued conceptually, not empirically verified (e.g. Bostrom's simulation argument) |
| **Metaphor** | Metaphorical or spiritual framing — useful for intuition, not proof |

When you read the agent, glance at the badge first: it tells you *what kind of statement*
you're looking at before you weigh it.

## Quick start

1. In the sidebar, open **Future Item Agents → Self-Simulating Reality Agent** (icon 🌀).
2. Start on **Overview** to meet the guiding phrase and the 5 levels.
3. Jump to **💬 Dialogue** to actually ask questions — this is the conversational agent.
4. Use **Core Concepts** and **Theory Tour** as reference for the ideas the Dialogue draws on.

The Dialogue and Claim Analyzer call a live AI model. If none is configured, they fall
back to a built-in example (marked **MOCK**) so the tabs still work — that label is your
honest signal that no model answered.

## The tabs

The module has 11 tabs — descriptive material, one conversation, and interactive tools.

### 🎯 Overview
Mission, the guiding phrase, and the 5 epistemic levels with descriptions. Start here.

### 📚 Core Concepts
Five OPH cards drawn from Mueller's presentation: the Self-Simulating Universe, the Past
Paradox, the **Observer Patch** (a bounded region with a state space that reads itself),
the Screen that encodes everything, and Modular Flow (how subjective time arises). All
tagged `speculative`.

### ⚙️ OPH Mechanics
The technical heart: the Strange Loop problem → Overlap Synchronization → the **Fact-Making
Pipeline** (local pattern → compare → repair → public fact) → Reality as a **fixed point**.
This is how OPH proposes that subjective patches generate objective physics by local
consensus rather than a global authority.

### 🧭 Theory Tour
Eight related theories, each with author, epistemic level and relation to OPH — Friston
(predictive processing), Rovelli (relational QM), 't Hooft/Susskind (holographic), Bostrom
(simulation), Tononi (IIT), Dehaene (GNW), Celestial Holography, and OPH itself. It lets
you see how much of OPH's "shape" already lives in accepted physics, and where it goes
beyond. Includes a Featured Voice card for physicist Sabrina Gonzalez Pasterski.

### 💬 Dialogue *(V1 — the conversational agent)*
Ask about observers, minds, consciousness, simulation, holography or OPH. **Every answer
comes back structured and tagged by evidence level**, not as loose prose:

- **Short answer** — one plain-language paragraph.
- **Sections** — scientific grounding / speculative extension / OPH interpretation, each
  with its own epistemic badge and sources.
- **Objections** — a built-in red-team critique of the agent's own answer.
- **Honest reformulation** — if your question contained an over-claim (e.g. "consciousness
  creates reality"), the agent rewrites it to keep both the solid and the speculative parts
  visible.
- **Suggested next question** — click it to keep exploring.

Answers are grounded in a **curated knowledge base** of OPH + science sources, each with
its own evidence level. Try the seed examples, or ask your own.

### 🔍 WiPhy Search
A live search over `wiphy.org` — physicist Pasterski's public corpus of physics papers and
claims. Use it to check what the literature actually says about a term.

### 🔬 Claim Analyzer
Paste a strong claim (e.g. *"consciousness collapses the wavefunction"*) and get it
decomposed into: an **epistemic verdict**, its **scientific core** (what has support and at
what level), its **overreach** (where it goes past the evidence, labelled by type), and a
**reformulation** that keeps both parts honest. Key terms become clickable chips that jump
to WiPhy Search.

### 🎨 Playground
Two intuition tools: a **Theory Map** (an interactive graph of OPH and its neighbours in
idea-space) and an **Observer Patch Simulator** (watch several patches reach consensus by
local overlap alone — a live demo of OPH's central claim). Both carry a disclaimer: they're
intuition pumps, not physics or consciousness.

### 🧠 AI as Observer
Five thought experiments about whether an AI could be an OPH observer patch — structurally
maybe (bounded context + state + self-referential outputs), phenomenologically unknown.
Every question is tagged `philosophy` and the tab carries an explicit warning that this is
speculation, not an AI-consciousness claim.

### 🌌 The Substrate Question
The philosophical climax: seven sections extending OPH's ontology — the hard problem,
substrate vs experience, the cosmological convergence with classical monism, the recursive
comprehension hypothesis, the Platonic question, the linguistic boundary, and three honest
positions you can hold. Each classical position is named with its year and primary work.

### 🗺️ Roadmap & Sources
Where the module has been and is going (V0 reading material → V1 Dialogue → V2 Claim
Analyzer → V3 Playground), plus the full reference list (OPH repo/book, Mueller, Bostrom,
Rovelli, Susskind, Friston, IIT, GNW, Pasterski). All external links open in a new tab.

## Frequently asked

**Is OPH real, accepted physics?**
No. OPH is always tagged **speculative** — a structured programme with its own formalism,
not consensus physics. The module is careful never to present it as established.

**Does "observer" mean a conscious mind?**
Not by default. In physics (e.g. relational QM) "observer" means any physical measuring
system. Conflating that with "conscious observer" is one of the main errors the agent
flags. Which one is meant depends on the claim — and the agent will tell you.

**Is the agent claiming AIs are conscious?**
No. The AI-as-Observer tab is explicitly philosophy/speculation, with a warning panel. It
sharpens the question; it does not answer it.

**What does "MOCK" mean on a Dialogue or Claim Analyzer answer?**
No AI model was configured, so you're seeing a built-in example instead of a live answer.
Configure a provider (e.g. OpenAI) in the app's API Config to get real responses.

**How is the Dialogue "grounded" — does it search the web?**
It grounds answers in a curated in-app knowledge base of OPH + science sources (not a live
web search). The WiPhy Search tab is the place to query the live physics literature.
